📖 Help & User Guide

Step-by-step instructions for using the Sunrise Dental Clinic Management System

👋 Welcome, New Administrator!

As the clinic administrator, you have full control over the Sunrise Dental system. This guide will walk you through the essential tasks you need to perform daily. From managing staff access to configuring clinic settings and generating revenue reports, everything is available in your secure dashboard.

👥 Staff User Management

  1. Go to "Staff Users" from the sidebar menu.
  2. To add a new staff member: fill in Full Name, Username, Password, and Role, then click "Create Account".
  3. To edit a staff member: click the ✏️ Edit button. Update the details in the popup and click "💾 Save Changes". Leave password blank to keep existing.
  4. To delete (deactivate) a staff member: click the 🗑️ Delete button. Confirm to disable their login access.
🔒

Only Administrators can create, edit, or deactivate staff accounts. There is no public self-registration. All changes are saved directly to the database.

⚙️ Clinic Settings

  1. Go to "Settings" to manage Treatments, Dentists, and the Consultation Fee.
  2. Add new treatment types with prices (e.g., Dental Cleaning — Rs. 4,500).
  3. Add new dentists with specialization. Update the consultation fee as needed.

📈 Reports

  1. Go to "Reports" to generate Daily Appointment Reports or Revenue Reports.
  2. Daily Report: Select a date to view all appointments for that day.
  3. Revenue Report: Select a date range to see total revenue by treatment type.

📅 All Appointments

  1. Go to "All Appointments" to view the complete appointment list.
  2. Use the date filter and dentist filter to narrow down results.

➕ Register New Appointment

  1. Click "Register Appointment" from your sidebar.
  2. Fill in the patient's Full Name, Address, and Contact Number (must be a valid Sri Lankan mobile number starting with 07, e.g., 0771234567).
  3. Select the Dentist and Treatment Type from the dropdown menus.
  4. Choose the Appointment Date (cannot be in the past) and Time (must be between 08:00 AM and 06:00 PM — clinic working hours).
  5. Click "Register Appointment". The system will generate a unique Appointment Number (e.g., APT0001). Share this number with the patient.
🔄

If the selected time slot is already booked for that dentist, the system will alert you to choose a different time. This prevents double bookings.

🔍 Search Appointment

  1. Navigate to "Search Appointment" from the sidebar.
  2. Enter the Appointment Number (e.g., APT0001) and click "Search".
  3. The system displays complete appointment details: patient name, address, contact, dentist, treatment, date, time, and current status.

🧾 Calculate & Print Bill

  1. Go to "Calculate & Print Bill" from the sidebar.
  2. Enter the Appointment Number and click "Calculate Bill".
  3. The bill shows: Treatment Cost + Consultation Fee = Total Amount, along with patient details (name, contact, address) and the treating dentist.
  4. Click "🖨️ Print Receipt" to print a professional receipt for the patient.

📋 Today's Appointments

  1. Click "Today's Appointments" to view all appointments scheduled for today.
  2. This helps you manage the patient queue and estimate waiting times.
  3. Appointments are sorted by time and show the status of each (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed).

📧 Send Reminder

  1. Navigate to "Send Reminder" from the sidebar.
  2. Enter the Appointment Number and select the reminder type: Email or SMS.
  3. Click "Send Reminder". The system will create a notification record and display a confirmation message.
📱

Reminders help reduce no-shows and improve patient attendance. Both Email and SMS notifications are supported by the system.

🦷 My Appointments Today

  1. Your Dashboard shows the number of patients scheduled for you today.
  2. Click "My Appointments Today" to see the full list with patient names, treatment types, times, and statuses.
  3. Use this to plan your day and prepare for each patient's treatment.

🔍 Search Appointment

  1. Navigate to "Search Appointment" from the sidebar.
  2. Enter an Appointment Number to look up the details of any patient assigned to you.
  3. View patient name, contact information, address, treatment type, and appointment status.

✏️ Update Appointment Status

  1. Go to "Update Status" from the sidebar.
  2. Enter the Appointment Number and select the new status.
  3. The workflow is: Scheduled → In Progress → Completed.
  4. Click "Update". The status change is immediately visible to the receptionist and admin.
🔒

You can only update the status of your own appointments. The system enforces this for security and accountability. All status changes are logged in the audit trail.
